From accident narratives to visual scenarios

Developed a pipeline that connects historical accident reports to structured hazard reasoning and visual safety scenarios using LLMs, scene graphs, diffusion models, and vision-language evaluation.

  1. Extract hazards and contextual relationships from accident narratives.
  2. Represent object-level spatial relationships as scene graphs.
  3. Generate visual scenarios guided by the structured representation.
  4. Evaluate semantic consistency between the intended hazard and the generated scene.

Public preprint: Scene Graph-Guided Generative AI Framework for Synthesizing and Evaluating Industrial Hazard Scenarios. arXiv:2511.13970, 2025.

The generated scenarios support research on safety training data. Synthetic scenarios require evaluation before use in a deployed hazard-detection system.
