Problem & platform

Construction environments change between visits. DeltaSeek studies how a robot's camera placement determines which changes it can observe, before deciding where to move next.

Husky A300 + UR5eROS 2GazeboRGB-DIFC

Methodology

  1. Convert a Revit IFC export into a room-scale benchmark with eight controlled changes.
  2. Evaluate visibility across 240 base poses: 60 positions at four yaw orientations.
  3. Combine each base pose with five arm postures for 1,200 wrist-camera configurations.
  4. Separate geometric observability from collision-aware base travel to an observation.

Results & limitations

The wrist camera observed two changes that were invisible from every chassis-camera pose. For mutually observable changes, median base travel was 6.0 m with wrist sensing and 15.2 m with chassis sensing.

These are geometric simulation results from one room and eight controlled changes. They do not establish robustness to sensor noise or physical-robot detection performance. Travel measures base translation, excluding arm motion and return travel.
