Research focus

Investigating floorplan reconstruction through field prediction and geometry-aware vectorization to recover structured spatial representations of the built environment.

The work connects learned spatial fields with geometric floorplan layouts, extending my research trajectory from 3D perception toward spatial understanding.

Manuscript: Floorplan Reconstruction through Field Prediction and Geometry-Aware Vectorization.

Status: Submitted to AAAI 2027.
