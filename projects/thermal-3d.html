Building geometry with thermal context

Fused drone-captured RGB and thermal imagery into dense 3D building profiles for spatially localized inspection and energy diagnostics.

417
Captured images
~10 million
Dense points

Reconstruction workflow

  1. Collect multimodal drone imagery of the building.
  2. Recover camera poses and sparse geometry with structure from motion in COLMAP.
  3. Map thermal information to sparse points and transfer it to a dense multi-view stereo reconstruction.
  4. Inspect spatially localized thermal patterns in the resulting point cloud.

This work provides a multimodal representation for inspection; automated defect detection and broader ground-truth evaluation are future directions.
