About Me

I am a Ph.D. researcher at the University of Texas at Arlington, working on robotic perception, active sensing, and mobile manipulation for construction and infrastructure. I am pursuing my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering and serve as a Graduate Research Assistant at the iSET Lab, advised by Dr. Md Nazmus Sakib.

My current work uses Husky A300 + UR5e and LeRobot SO-101 platforms to connect perception with robot action. I develop embodiment-aware sensing, analytical kinematics, and closed-loop visual servoing, combining ROS 2, simulation, computer vision, and experiments on physical hardware. My 3D perception research also combines RGB and thermal imagery, point clouds, and spatial representations for building inspection.

I am seeking Summer 2027 internships in robotics and Physical AI, particularly in field robotics, perception, autonomy, mobile manipulation, and real-world deployment.
